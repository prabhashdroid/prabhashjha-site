> You do not need a website to start affiliate marketing. You need an audience — even a small one — and a place to publish honest recommendations. This is exactly how I'd start from zero today, without paying for a website or a single tool.

## Can you really do affiliate marketing without a website?

Yes. A website helps, but it isn't the entry ticket. What actually earns commissions is **trust plus reach**, and you can build both on platforms you don't own yet. Starting without a website just means you skip the setup and get to the real skill faster — recommending things people actually buy.

## Where to do it without a website

- **YouTube** — reviews and “best of” videos. High trust, high intent, and the description holds your links.

- **Instagram or short video** — reels and stories, with a link in bio or an in-app link tool.

- **A free newsletter** — you own the audience even without a website. This is the one I'd protect most.

- **Communities** — answering real questions on Quora, Reddit or niche forums, where a genuinely helpful reply can carry a link.

- **Free publishing platforms** — Medium or LinkedIn articles to hold your longer, honest reviews.

## The approach that actually works

Pick one platform and one niche you can be useful in. Recommend only what you'd stake your name on. Be honest — including the downsides — because honesty is what makes a stranger trust your link. Then track which recommendations actually convert, and do more of that. That's the whole game, website or not.

## Step-by-step: start this week

1. **Pick one niche** you know something about and **one platform** you'll actually keep using.

1. **Join 2–3 affiliate programs** in that niche and get your links.

1. **Create buyer-intent content** — “best X for Y”, an honest review, or “how I actually use it”.

1. **Disclose your affiliate links** clearly. It's required in many places, and it builds trust.

1. **Start a free email list** from day one — an audience you own, no website needed.

1. **Track what converts**, cut what doesn't, and repeat.

## Mistakes to skip

- Spraying links with no trust behind them — it never works.

- Promoting things you haven't used or wouldn't recommend to a friend.

- Building only on a platform you don't own — always grow an email list alongside.

- Chasing follower counts instead of buyer intent.

## FAQs

### Do I need money to start affiliate marketing without a website?

No. Content-based affiliate marketing on YouTube, social or a free newsletter costs time, not money. Paid ads need a budget and proven numbers first.

### Is a website better in the long run?

Yes. Once you know what works, a website (SEO) is the most durable channel because it compounds and you own it. Start without one; graduate to one.

### Which platform is best without a website?

Whichever you'll actually keep doing for a year. YouTube and a free newsletter are my top picks — one for trust, one for ownership.

## Key takeaways

- A website helps but isn't required — trust plus reach earns commissions.

- Start on YouTube, social, communities or a free newsletter.

- Own an email list from day one, even without a site.

- Recommend only what you'd stake your name on, and track what converts.
