I learnt, Networking is the key to individual, and business success. It helps people find a new opportunities, hire people, find customer, suppliers, and in many cases buyers for business or initiatives.

## I found out networking is easier when...

1. I was approachable, and happy.

1. Had a reason to initiate a conversation with my bosses, peers, and juniors.

1. Reason mentioned above was not always work related. It was anything which people would love to talk about. Movies, show, even daily soaps ;)

1. Never asked for favour, or any kind of action which only benefited me. Unless, I already helped the subject person.

## Benefit from Networking

1. Healthy banter brings peace, and happiness empowering mental health. One of the must for corporate success.

1. Deepens bonds with colleagues, acquaintances, and close friends.

1. Helps you gain new insights, information that can be essential to your growth.

1. Raises one's overall profile and visibility.
