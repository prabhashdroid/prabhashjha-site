I'm Prabhash Jha, co-founder of ADVOLT. Eight years ago I couldn't find honest, practical answers about marketing and building a business — so I'm writing them.

I trained as an engineer and got into digital marketing almost by accident, then discovered I liked the game of turning attention into demand, and demand into revenue. Over the next 8+ years I worked across performance marketing, programmatic and affiliate — running growth for streaming, e-commerce, D2C, insurance and BFSI brands across India and South East Asia.

## Why this site exists

Most marketing content is written to rank, not to help. It explains what a term means and stops exactly where the useful part begins. I wanted the opposite: the frameworks I actually use, the mistakes that cost me money, and the numbers that decide whether something works.

> Profit = (LTV − CAC) × Volume. Fix what's inside the bracket before you touch the volume.

## What I write about

- **Performance & affiliate** — media buying, tracking, and scaling paid profitably.
- **Digital marketing** — SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, programmatic, analytics.
- **Brand building** — positioning and demand that compounds.
- **Business & finance** — cash flow, pricing, margins, unit economics.
- **AI & automation** — using AI as leverage in a real business.
